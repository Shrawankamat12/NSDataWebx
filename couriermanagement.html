About Our Courier Management System

Efficient, Reliable, and Real-Time Courier Tracking.

The software we provide is Courier Management Software and it is developed to meet all the requirements of courier and logistics businesses. This advanced solution provides an effective means for tracking the deliveries with simple and easy-to-use features. Created to increase productivity, the software helps in the proper distribution of the packet, effective reporting, and billing. It offers full reporting and control of the business so that the top, middle, and lower management can get a clear & transparent visibility of the entire operations. Therefore, through our cutting-edge software, courier companies stand to benefit from increased operational efficiency and accuracy in reporting, as well as better service delivery, all of which will help to facilitate business expansion and customer satisfaction.

Features and Functions of Courier Management Software

Maximize Efficiency with Smart Courier Management.

Real-Time Tracking

Track shipments live with GPS integration, allowing customers and managers to monitor parcel locations and estimated delivery times.

Automated Notifications

Send instant SMS or email alerts for pickup, dispatch, and delivery status, keeping customers informed and engaged.

Inventory Management

Maintain records of shipments, parcels in transit, and returned items. Helps avoid lost packages and improves stock control.

Billing & Invoicing

Generate automated invoices and receipts for completed shipments with tax and discount calculations, reducing manual errors.

Driver Management

Assign, track, and evaluate driver performance. Provides tools for scheduling shifts and monitoring driving efficiency.

Multi-Location Support

Manage multiple courier branches from one platform, ensuring centralized monitoring and smooth inter-branch coordination.

Customer Portal

Allow customers to book shipments, track parcels, and download invoices directly from a secure self-service portal.

Return Management

Simplify handling of returned parcels by automating pickup scheduling, customer refunds, and stock adjustments.
