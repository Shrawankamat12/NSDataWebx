About Our Pickup & Delivery Management System

Smart, Reliable, and Real-Time Delivery Management.

Our Pickup & Delivery Management System is built to simplify order fulfillment and last-mile logistics. This powerful solution enables businesses to automate order assignments, track drivers in real-time, optimize delivery routes, and provide proof of delivery with digital signatures or photos. Designed for speed and accuracy, it ensures seamless coordination between pickup requests, dispatch teams, and delivery agents. With complete visibility and centralized control, businesses can reduce delivery delays, lower operational costs, and improve customer satisfaction. Whether managing e-commerce shipments, courier services, or on-demand deliveries, our system ensures efficiency, reliability, and scalability for modern businesses.

Features of Pickup & Delivery Management Software

Streamline Your Pickup and Last-Mile Delivery Operations.

Smart Order Assignment

Automatically assign pickup and delivery requests to the nearest available driver. Prioritize urgent tasks and optimize routes for faster response. Reduce manual dispatching errors with automation. Improve overall delivery efficiency.

Driver & Fleet Tracking

Monitor drivers and vehicles in real time with GPS. Optimize delivery routes to save fuel and time. Track driver performance and workloads. Ensure better fleet utilization and reduced idle time.

Real-Time Tracking & Notifications

Provide customers with live tracking links and accurate ETAs. Send instant notifications for pickup, in-transit, and completed deliveries. Increase customer trust with transparency. Reduce support queries and complaints.

Proof of Delivery

Enable digital signatures, OTPs, or photo confirmation. Securely record all delivery details in the system. Eliminate disputes with verified proof of handovers. Enhance customer satisfaction and accountability.

Integrated Payments & Billing

Automate invoice generation for completed deliveries. Accept multiple payment modes (cash, card, wallet, UPI). Ensure secure, transparent transactions. Simplify finance tracking and reconciliations.

Analytics & Performance Insights

Track delivery success rates, turnaround time, and driver productivity. Identify bottlenecks in operations quickly. Generate detailed cost and efficiency reports. Make smarter, data-driven business decisions.
